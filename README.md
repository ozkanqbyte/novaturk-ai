## 🌟 Genel Bakış

**AiPremium Hesap Makinesi**, geleneksel hesaplama ile son teknoloji yapay zekayı birleştiren devrim niteliğinde bir hesap makinesi uygulamasıdır. Flutter ile geliştirilmiş olup, ses komutları, kamera tabanlı problem çözme ve akıllı açıklamalarla benzersiz bir matematik deneyimi sunar.

## ✨ Features

### 🎯 Core Functionality
- **Basic Calculator**: All standard arithmetic operations
- **Scientific Calculator**: Advanced mathematical functions (trigonometry, logarithms, etc.)
- **AI-Powered Calculations**: Natural language processing for math problems
- **Voice Input**: Speak your math problems naturally
- **Camera Solve**: Take photos of handwritten or printed math problems
- **Step-by-Step Solutions**: Detailed explanations of problem-solving processes

### 🤖 AI Features
- **Natural Language Processing**: "What is 15 percent of 200?" → Instant solution
- **Voice Recognition**: Multilingual support with continuous listening
- **Optical Character Recognition (OCR)**: Extract math from images
- **Intelligent Explanations**: Learn how problems are solved
- **Cultural Math Approaches**: Different mathematical perspectives from around the world

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Modern Dark Theme**: Eye-friendly design optimized for all lighting conditions
- **Smooth Animations**: Fluid transitions and micro-interactions
- **Customizable Interface**: Personalize colors, themes, and layouts
- **Gesture Support**: Swipe, pinch, and tap for intuitive navigation

### 📊 Smart Features
- **Calculation History**: Never lose your work with persistent storage
- **Favorites System**: Save important calculations for quick access
- **Search Functionality**: Find past calculations instantly
- **Social Sharing**: Share solutions as beautiful visual stories
- **Progress Tracking**: Gamification with points, levels, and achievements

### 🌍 Accessibility
- **Multi-language Support**: Voice commands in multiple languages
- **Text-to-Speech**: Hear your results spoken aloud
- **Large Text Options**: Accessibility-friendly font sizes
- **Color Contrast**: High contrast mode for better visibility
